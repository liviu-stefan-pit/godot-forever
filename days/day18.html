Concepts you need first

Offline is just elapsed time times rate

On load, and again when the app resumes, elapsed seconds are int(Time.get_unix_time_from_system()) - saved_unix. Negative values (clock stepped backward) become 0. The grant uses

earned = rate * min(elapsed, 28800) * 0.5

28800 seconds is 8 hours. The 0.5 efficiency means an idle phone never beats someone who is tapping. Worked example: one sprout, rate 0.1, away 7200 seconds (2 hours). 0.1 * 7200 * 0.5 = 360 crumbs. Away 10 hours is clamped to 8 hours: 0.1 * 28800 * 0.5 = 1440 crumbs. Grants under 5 seconds are ignored so an autosave does not pop the panel.

The save writes saved_unix inside your data dictionary. SaveManager's own saved_at wrapper is not what this game reads. Focus-out and NOTIFICATION_APPLICATION_PAUSED both save, which matters on Android where the OS pauses the process instead of quitting it. Resume grants the gap once, then saves again so a second notification cannot pay twice.

Sparks

Lifetime crumbs never reset. Total sparks ever earned are

floorf(sqrt(lifetime / 1_000_000))

So 1,000,000 lifetime crumbs is 1 spark, 4,000,000 is 2, and 100,000,000 is 10. The button pays total - sparks_claimed and then sets claimed equal to total. Unspent sparks each multiply global production by an extra 0.02: 1 + 0.02 * unspent. Spending a spark on a permanent upgrade removes that 2% and keeps the upgrade across prestiges. Crumbs, generator counts, and upgrades with Persists off are cleared. Achievements stay.

Ads and IAP are a callback, not a scene

A rewarded ad and a real-money product both end in a function you already trust: add crumbs, or grant an upgrade without charging sparks. This course wires a clearly labeled debug button to grant_debug_minute() so you can test that path. It does not embed an ad network or a billing plugin. Those change often and they add a privacy disclosure. The callout in Block 5 names the official sites.

Common pitfalls

  • Editing the wrapper timestamp instead of data.saved_unix does nothing. This game never reads the wrapper.
  • Two resume notifications double-paying. _grant_elapsed ignores gaps under 5 seconds, and the first resume calls save_run(), which moves the anchor. Keep both.
  • Prestige clearing Ledger. Persists must be on, and Pay must be Sparks. A crumbs upgrade with Persists off is removed on purpose.
  • Spending sparks still expecting +2% each. The 2% applies to unspent sparks only. Ledger at 1.10 is better than one unspent spark at 1.02, which is why the trade exists.
  • Export With Debug left on for the AAB. Play closed testing wants the release keystore and a non-debug export. Day 16 separates the two keystores.
  • Unique Name missing on a node you added today fails at _ready before the first tap. The error names the missing node.
  • Replacing game.gd before the new nodes exist produces a pile of unique-name errors. Build the tree, then paste the script.

Check yourself

Rate is 100 crumbs per second and the app was closed for 10 hours. How many crumbs does the offline grant add?

  • 100 * 36000 = 3,600,000
  • 100 * 28800 * 0.5 = 1,440,000
  • 100 * 28800 = 2,880,000
  • 100 * 0.5 = 50

Elapsed is clamped to 8 hours (28800 seconds) and then multiplied by 0.5. Ten hours does not pay the extra two.

Lifetime is 4,000,000 and you have never prestiged. How many sparks does this prestige pay?

  • 4
  • 40
  • 2
  • 1

floorf(sqrt(4e6 / 1e6)) = floorf(sqrt(4)) = 2. The self-check print is this formula.

You own Ledger (global ×1.10, persists) and have 0 unspent sparks. What is the global multiplier from sparks and Ledger together?

  • 1.10
  • 1.12
  • 1.02
  • 1.00, because prestige cleared Ledger

Unspent sparks add 0. Spent sparks do not. Ledger persists and multiplies. 1.00 + 0, then ×1.10.

Which notification is the one Android sends when the player switches away, and which this game uses to save?

  • NOTIFICATION_WM_CLOSE_REQUEST only
  • Events.game_paused
  • NOTIFICATION_TRANSLATION_CHANGED
  • NOTIFICATION_APPLICATION_PAUSED (and focus-out)

The economy _notification saves on application pause and on focus-out, then SaveManager.flush() inside save_run.
