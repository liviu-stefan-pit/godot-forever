Concepts you need first

One collision, two signals

body_entered fires on both RigidBody2D nodes. If each one spawns the next fruit, one pair becomes two, and those two immediately merge again. The fix is synchronous and uses no timer: both scripts set merging = true on both bodies before any await, and only the body with the lower get_instance_id() emits merge_requested. The other returns as soon as it sees the flag. Collision layers are cleared with set_deferred, because changing physics state inside a physics callback is unsafe. The new fruit is spawned with call_deferred for the same reason.

The jar, in pixels

The playfield is 720 by 1280, the same as the viewport. Y grows downward. Inner walls sit at x = 48 and x = 672, so the gap is 624 px. The floor surface is y = 1168. Fruits are dropped with their center at y = 150. The danger line is y = 250. A fruit whose center stays above that line (a smaller y) for 2.0 seconds after its grace period loses. Dropped fruits get 0.75 seconds of grace so the drop itself is not an instant loss. Merged fruits get 0.4 seconds.

Radius is round(18 * 1.22 ^ tier) and mass is 0.35 * 1.25 ^ tier, rounded to two decimals. Those expressions are how the table was chosen. The game reads the table, not pow, so every machine uses the same circle. The largest fruit has radius 131 (diameter 262), which fits in the 624 px gap. Two of them fit side by side. Score for producing tier t is t * t * 2, multiplied by the combo. Combo starts at 1 and increases by 1 when another merge happens within 1.5 seconds.

Project gravity is 1600 px/s² straight down, not the default 980, so a fruit dropped at y = 150 reaches the danger line in about a third of a second and the floor in a bit over a second. Each body replaces linear damp with 0.8 and angular damp with 2.0 so the stack sleeps instead of shivering. Shared material: friction 0.40, bounce 0.12. Continuous CD is CCD_MODE_CAST_SHAPE so a fast drop does not tunnel through the floor.

Coming from Unity

This is a dynamic rigidbody, not a kinematic character. You do not call move_and_slide(). You set mass, damp, and a shape, then let the solver move it. The preview ghost is not a body at all.

Shared shapes

If you duplicate a CircleShape2D and then change its radius, every fruit that points at that same sub-resource changes size. setup() assigns CircleShape2D.new() so that cannot happen. Do not "optimize" it by editing the scene shape at runtime.

Common pitfalls

  • Both bodies spawn. The instance-id test and the merging flag have to run before queue_free, with no await in between. A deferred spawn of the result is still one spawn.
  • Mask on the wrong bit. Walls mask pickups (layer 6). Fruits mask world and pickups (collision_mask = 33). Two fruits that pass through each other are both on mask world only.
  • Editing the scene's CircleShape2D radius resizes every fruit that still shares it. setup() replaces the shape. Keep that line.
  • Scaling Fruit itself scales the collider and the mass distribution. The pop tween scales Visual only.
  • Danger uses the drop point. Center y = 150 is above the line y = 250, so without grace the drop would lose immediately. Do not set grace to 0.
  • HudRoot mouse Filter left on Stop eats every click and the ghost never drops. The root ignores; the game-over container stops.
  • Running F6 on fruit.tscn after you meant to test the jar. Check the window title and the scene tab.

Check yourself

Two lemons (tier 4) touch. Who spawns the apple, and what is the base score before combo?

  • Both spawn an apple, score 32 each.
  • Only the lower get_instance_id() spawns one apple (tier 5). Base score is 50.
  • The heavier one spawns it. Base score is 32.
  • Neither, because lemons are tier 4 and only tier 0 merges.

Score is for the fruit that appears: 5 * 5 * 2 = 50. The other body sees merging and returns. Planet (tier 10) is the one that refuses to merge.

Why set contact_monitor and max_contacts_reported?

  • They turn on gravity.
  • They are required for move_and_slide().
  • body_entered is not emitted unless contact monitor is on and the reported-contact count is above 0.
  • They replace the collision shape.

The fruit uses 6 reported contacts. Monitor defaults to off, and the max defaults to 0, which records nothing.

Why is the merged fruit created with call_deferred?

  • Adding a rigid body inside a physics callback is unsafe; defer it to after the step.
  • Deferred calls run on a network peer.
  • It makes the merge happen twice, which the combo wants.
  • It converts the body into a character body.

The same rule is why collision_layer is changed with set_deferred rather than assigned directly in body_entered.

A grape is dropped at center y = 150 and the danger line is y = 250. Why is that not an instant loss?

  • Danger checks the floor, not the line.
  • Gravity is 0 until the first merge.
  • The line only sees planets.
  • The new fruit has 0.75 seconds of grace, and the loss needs 2.0 seconds above the line after that.

Smaller y is higher on the screen. The drop starts above the line on purpose. Grace covers the fall through it.

What is the watermelon radius and mass in the table this game actually uses?

  • Radius 100, mass 10, because round numbers are easier.
  • Radius 108, mass 2.61.
  • Radius 131, mass 0.35.
  • Whatever pow returns on that phone, recomputed every launch.

Tier 9 is stored as 108 and 2.61. The formula explains the table; the array is what the collider uses, so two machines do not disagree about a rounding edge.
