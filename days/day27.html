Concepts you need first

The loop

Deep Digger is an endless extraction miner. A shaft drops through a dirt crust. Below that, noise decides air, dirt, stone, ore, and bedrock. You fly in open cells, drill solid cells, and spend fuel to do it. Ore occupies cargo. Dirt and stone do not. The surface refills the tank. Selling and upgrades are Day 28.

FastNoiseLite is a Resource, not a node

If you have called Perlin noise from another engine, this is the same idea. FastNoiseLite extends Noise. get_noise_2d(x, y) returns a float, about −1 to 1. frequency is the zoom: low values (we use 0.045 for caves) change slowly, high values flicker cell to cell. fractal_type of FRACTAL_FBM stacks octaves. The seed makes the same hole appear on the next launch. Sample with the cell coordinate. Do not also multiply the coordinate by the frequency, or you scale the noise twice.

TileMapLayer, one cell at a time

A chunk is a 16×16 block of cells, the same idea as a streaming sector. set_cell(coords, source_id, atlas_coords, alternative_tile) paints one cell. erase_cell(coords) removes the tile and its collision. local_to_map expects a position in the TileMapLayer's own space, so a global drill position goes through to_local first. map_to_local returns the center of a cell. Add a TileMapLayer node. Do not add the older tile-grid node Godot deprecated in favor of TileMapLayer.

Docs checked for this day

FastNoiseLite frequency, seed, TYPE_SIMPLEX_SMOOTH, FRACTAL_FBM. Noise.get_noise_2d. TileMapLayer.set_cell, erase_cell, local_to_map, map_to_local. TileSet creation and the F rectangle collision shortcut are in the tileset tutorial.

Block 4: Drill, fuel, cargo, HUD

Fuel per second while you thrust or while the dig timer is running:

(1.6 + 0.035 * depth) * (1 - 0.08 * efficiency_level)

depth is max(0, cell_y - 2). Efficiency is 0 today, so a full tank of 100 lasts about 62 seconds of thrusting at the surface and about 33 seconds at depth 40. Holding still does not burn fuel. At the surface (cell_y <= 2) the tank refills at 40 per second. Out of fuel, speed drops from 110 to 44 and the drill will not bite.

Common pitfalls

  • Tile size edited after the atlas existed. The grid no longer matches 16 pixels. Delete the atlas and drag tiles.png again.
  • Passing global coordinates to local_to_map. The drill looks one cell off and digs the wrong tile. Convert with to_local first.
  • Integer division for negative chunks. -1 / 16 in GDScript is 0. _chunk_of uses floori on floats so the cell left of the origin stays in chunk −1.
  • Collision on magma and gas. Those two atlas columns must not have a physics rectangle. Tomorrow they are volumes you enter.
  • Circle radius 16. The body is wider than a cell and sticks in the shaft. Radius is 6.
  • Hand-painted tiles. Code only overwrites chunks it loads. If you painted with the bottom panel's tile-painting tab, choose the Eraser there and wipe those cells.
  • Edited an autoload. Fuel and cargo live on game.gd. SaveManager is not involved until tomorrow.

Check yourself

What does FastNoiseLite.frequency do?

  • It is the random seed.
  • Low values make smooth regions; high values make finer detail.
  • It sets the TileSet tile size in pixels.
  • It picks the atlas source id.

frequency scales every noise type. The seed is a separate property. This project uses 0.045 for caves and 0.09 for ore.

How do you remove one mined cell, including its collision?

  • erase_cell(coords) on the TileMapLayer.
  • Hide the whole TileMapLayer.
  • queue_free() on the TileMapLayer.
  • Set the camera limit so the cell is off screen.

erase_cell clears the source id, atlas coords, and the physics shape for that cell. set_cell with source id -1 erases too. The project stores the coordinate in _dug so a later chunk load does not put the rock back.

The drill's global_position is known. What do you pass to local_to_map?

  • The global position, unchanged.
  • The position divided by the viewport size.
  • terrain.to_local(drill.global_position).
  • terrain.map_to_local(drill.global_position).

local_to_map expects the TileMapLayer's local space. map_to_local goes the other way and returns a cell center.

Why is the drill's Motion Mode set to Floating?

  • So gravity pulls the drill down the shaft.
  • So collisions are walls in every direction, with no floor snap.
  • So move_and_slide can take a velocity argument.
  • So the body ignores the TileMapLayer.

The miner flies. Floating mode stops Godot from treating downward collisions as floors. move_and_slide() still uses the velocity property and takes no arguments.

Which tiles add one cargo unit when they break?

  • Dirt, stone, and bedrock.
  • Every solid tile.
  • Only crystal.
  • Copper, iron, gold, and crystal, and only when the bay is not full.

Dirt and stone are path. Bedrock never breaks. Ore adds one unit at values 8, 18, 45, and 120. A full bay (20 today) refuses another ore tile.
