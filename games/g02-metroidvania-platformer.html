Cave Runner

A two-room cave in one sitting. You run, jump, and later dash and double-jump, but only after the cave hands you those moves. Slimes and spike tiles take hearts. Coins and the last checkpoint are written to disk. The exit of room 1 is room 2, and room 2 ends when you touch the goal.

Built on Days 5–7TileMapLayer, CharacterBody2D, AnimationPlayer, Parallax2D, PointLight2D

Game design

Cave Runner is a small Metroidvania: the map is short, but two moves are locked behind pickups, and the rooms assume you have them. It is not an open world. It is the smallest game that still has ability gates, a checkpoint, and a save file.

Core loop

  1. Cross room 1 with the base jump (coyote time, jump buffer, variable height).
  2. Take the cyan orb (dash) on the big block, then the gold orb (double_jump) past the pit.
  3. Walk through the opening in the right wall into room 2.
  4. Climb the two platforms. The green goal ends the run and shows the coin total.

Dying at zero hearts is not a game-over screen. GameState refills health and the game reloads the checkpoint room at the checkpoint position. Quit and relaunch does the same, because that fact is in user://cave_runner.json.

Win, fail, score

  • Win: the player body overlaps Goal in room 2. The tree pauses. The win screen shows Coins: N.
  • Fail a room: health hits 0. Respawn at the checkpoint, health back to 3. Abilities and coins stay.
  • Score: coin count only. No timer. Restart from the win screen calls GameState.reset_run(), which writes a fresh save.

Scope: what "done" means

Training-game done is this list, not a store build. Day 7's wrap-up is the same list in playable form. Tick these only when you have seen them in a running project.

  • Project C:\GodotProjects\g02-cave-runner is 1152x648, stretch mode canvas_items, aspect keep, Mobile renderer, default texture filter Nearest
  • 2D physics layers are named world, player, enemies, player_attacks, enemy_attacks, pickups, triggers
  • Input actions exist: move_left, move_right, jump, dash, pause
  • res://tiles/cave_tileset.tres has a 32 px atlas, physics on rock, the one-way plank and the spikes, terrain set Rock (Match Sides), and custom data damage = 1 on the spike tile
  • room_01.tscn root is Room01 with TileMapLayer nodes Background, Decor, Ground, Hazards (Hazards quadrant size 1)
  • The player is a CharacterBody2D in group player, mask world only, with coyote time, jump buffer, variable jump height and a heavier fall
  • Movement is one enum + match state machine: IDLE, RUN, JUMP, FALL, DASH, HURT. One move_and_slide() per physics frame
  • AnimationPlayer plays idle, run, jump, fall, dash, hurt from player_sheet.png (10 frames of 32x40)
  • Dash runs only when GameState.has_dash is true, granted by the orb whose ability_id is dash
  • The extra air jump runs only when GameState.has_double_jump is true, granted by ability_id double_jump
  • The player's Hurtbox (mask enemy_attacks) takes damage from a slime Hitbox (layer enemy_attacks): knockback plus an invulnerability flicker
  • Spike tiles deal damage through TileData.get_custom_data("damage") after move_and_slide()
  • The slime patrols with FloorRay and WallRay, both masked to world, and turns at ledges and walls
  • Coins add to the counter and stay collected across room loads because each instance has a unique pickup_id
  • Three hearts and the coin label sit on HeartsHud (CanvasLayer), nodes Heart1, Heart2, Heart3, CoinLabel
  • CaveRoom sets camera limits and smoothing: room 1 is 1536x672 at speed 8, room 2 is 1280x512 at speed 12
  • ParallaxFar and ParallaxMid are Parallax2D nodes (scroll scales 0.2 and 0.45, repeat 1152x720). No ParallaxBackground
  • CanvasModulate darkens the cave, the player's Lantern is a PointLight2D using light.png with shadows on, and at least one LightOccluder2D casts a shadow
  • The Exit in room 1 loads res://rooms/room_02.tscn at spawn (128, 396) without dropping coins or abilities
  • Autoload node name GameState saves coins, health, abilities, collected ids and the checkpoint to user://cave_runner.json, and load_game casts coins and health with int()
  • A Checkpoint updates that spawn. Dying reloads the checkpoint room there and refills health
  • Esc opens PauseMenu (Process Mode Always). Resume unpauses. Quit saves and closes
  • Goal in room 2 shows WinScreen (group win_screen) with the coin total. Restart calls reset_run() and reloads game.tscn

Architecture

The main scene owns everything that must survive a room swap. Rooms are instanced under World and freed on the way out. GameState is an autoload, so it is not freed when game.tscn reloads either; reset_run() clears it on purpose.

Stretch goals

  • Stomp. The Day 6 challenge: falling on a slime bounces you and frees the slime, using frame 2 of slime_sheet.png. Keep the hurtbox from also applying damage on that path.
  • Dash i-frames. Set _invuln_timer for the dash duration so a spike strip becomes a dash gate. The rooms still completable without it; this only changes the feel.
  • Tile occluders. Add an occlusion layer to cave_tileset.tres and paint a full-tile polygon on the 16 rock tiles (same F shortcut as collision, under Rendering). Then delete the hand-placed LightOccluder2D nodes. The Day 7 docs link covers the editor.
  • Kenney art swap. See Assets. Do it last; coordinates in Days 5-7 match the generated sheet only.
  • A third room behind a floor that is only crossable with both abilities. Copy room_02.tscn, give it a new scene_path, and point a second Exit at it. Reuse CaveRoom; do not fork the camera code.
  • Collected-id audit. Print GameState.collected from the pause menu so a missed pickup_id (two coins sharing one id) shows up before you trust the save.
