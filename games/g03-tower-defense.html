Bastion Defense

A fixed-lane tower defense. Enemies walk a hand-drawn path, leaks cost lives, and you spend gold on four towers with three levels each. Clear eight waves or lose the base. The only persistent number is the best wave you have cleared.

Built on Days 8–9TileMapLayer, Path2D, Resource, Area2D

Game design

The player never steers a character. The skill is where a tower sits and when you can afford the next one. Enemies enter off the left edge, follow GroundPath (or AirPath if they fly), and either die or reach the base at cell (14, 6). The route does not change when you build. Grass is the only legal foundation; path and rock are not.

Controls. Mouse only. Left-click a build button, then left-click a grass cell. Left-click an existing tower to select it. Right-click cancels placement and clears the selection. The bottom bar upgrades, sells, cycles targeting (First, Last, Closest, Strongest), and toggles speed. Restart is on the end overlay. There is no keyboard map.

Win. All eight waves of level_01.tres spawn and leave the map while lives are still above 0. The Victory overlay pauses the tree.

Lose. Lives hit 0. The current wave pays no clear bonus and does not count as cleared. Restart reloads the scene at 1× speed without wiping the best-wave record.

Economy. Start at 150 gold and 20 lives. A kill pays that enemy's reward. A leak subtracts damage_to_base and pays nothing. When a wave is empty, you gain its clear_bonus plus 10% of the gold you hold at that moment, truncated, capped at 50. Selling returns int(spent * 0.6) for that tower's build plus upgrades. One tower per cell.

Tower prices and combat stats (damage, range, cooldown, shot speed, splash radius, slow factor, slow duration) are the twelve-row table in Day 9. Arrow is the opener (50 gold, 15 damage, 190 px). Cannon is full-damage splash. Frost slows (level 3 also splashes). Sniper is the long-range single shot. A measured opening: one Arrow on cell (2, 3) that kills all six wave-1 grunts with no leaks ends that wave at 168 gold.

Scope: what "done" means

Training-game done is the list below, all of it in C:\GodotProjects\g03-bastion-defense. Days 8 and 9 are the build. This page is the contract those days fulfill.

  • 16×8 map on a TileMapLayer, 72 px tiles, buildable custom data true only on grass
  • GroundPath (eight points) and AirPath (spawn to base) under Paths
  • Five EnemyData resources with the stats in the design table
  • Enemies are PathFollow2D children of the matching path; a leak emits reached_end and costs lives
  • Kills pay reward; damaged enemies draw a health bar and a hit flash; slowed enemies draw a blue ring
  • WaveGroup, WaveData and LevelData, with level_01.tres holding eight waves
  • WaveSpawner awaits prep and spawn gaps with create_timer(..., false) so pause freezes the wave
  • Start at 150 gold and 20 lives; a clear pays clear_bonus plus 10% interest capped at 50
  • Game Over overlay pauses the tree; Restart reloads the scene
  • Four TowerData resources, three TowerLevel rows each, matching Day 9's numbers
  • Placement uses local_to_map, refuses non-buildable cells, and allows one tower per cell
  • Targeting cycles First, Last, Closest and Strongest
  • Projectiles and tower ranges use layer 4 (player_attacks) and mask layer 3 (enemies)
  • Cannon deals full splash damage; Frost applies apply_slow; Frost level 3 also splashes
  • Upgrade spends the next level's cost; Sell refunds 60% of gold spent on that tower, rounded down
  • Speed toggles Engine.time_scale between 1 and 2, and a new run starts at 1
  • Victory overlay after wave 8 if lives remain
  • Best cleared wave is stored in user://best_wave.json and shown on the HUD across restarts

Physics layers, named in Project Settings: 1 world, 2 player, 3 enemies, 4 player_attacks, 5 enemy_attacks, 6 pickups, 7 triggers. Enemy hitboxes are layer 3, mask empty, monitoring off. Tower ranges and projectiles are layer 4, mask layer 3. In code those bits are 4 and 8. Groups used: enemies, towers, projectiles.
