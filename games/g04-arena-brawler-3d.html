Arena Brawler 3D

A third-person brawler in a CSG bowl. You orbit a spring-arm camera, swing light and heavy attacks, dodge with a short invulnerable window, and clear four waves of navigation enemies, one of which throws projectiles. The run ends on a pause menu or a win/lose card.

Built on Days 10–11CharacterBody3D, SpringArm3D, AnimationTree, NavigationAgent3D, Area3D

Game design

One arena, one life, four waves. Between waves the game waits 2.5 seconds. There is no shop and no level-up. The skill is spacing: the light swing is fast and short, the heavy swing is a commitment, and the dodge's i-frames end before the dash does.

Scope: what "done" means

This is a training game. Done means the list below, played from F5 with no test enemies left in the main scene. It is not a store build: no menus beyond pause and game over, no save, no localization.

  • Project C:\GodotProjects\g04-arena-brawler-3d, Forward+, res://game/main.tscn is the main scene.
  • CSG arena: Arena combiner, Base and subtractive Hollow, four pillars, Dais, Ramp, two blocks, use_collision only on the root.
  • Materials mat_stone, mat_accent, mat_dais saved under res://game/.
  • WorldEnvironment (procedural sky, filmic tonemap, glow, SSAO, depth fog) and Sun with shadows on, max distance 40.
  • art/player.glb and art/mob.glb imported from the official starter zip. No attack clips are required from the files.
  • 3D physics layers named world, player, enemies, player_attacks, enemy_attacks, pickups, triggers.
  • Input Map: move_left, move_right, move_up, move_down, jump, dash, attack, heavy_attack, look_left, look_right, look_up, look_down, pause.
  • Player is a CharacterBody3D with capsule, Pivot/Rig/Model, and CameraYaw/CameraPitch/SpringArm3D/Camera3D. Mouse and stick orbit. Movement is camera-relative. Jump lands on the ramp and the dais.
  • class_name Health on the player (100), each chaser (40), and each spitter (28).
  • Six authored clips: idle, run, jump, attack, heavy_attack, dodge. Swings key Rig. Bob and hop key Pivot position.
  • AnimationTree state machine travels from code. Attack, heavy, and dodge return to idle with Advance Mode Auto and Switch Mode At End.
  • Player AttackHitbox is an Area3D on player_attacks scanning enemies, open only during the hit window, one hit per enemy per swing.
  • Dodge: 11 m/s, 0.45 s, i-frames 0.28 s, cooldown 0.55 s.
  • Navigation mesh baked from the CSG static collider. Chasers use NavigationAgent3D and call get_next_path_position() every physics frame.
  • Spitters keep about 8 m and fire projectile.tscn (10 damage, 11 m/s, 2.5 s). Shots die on the player and on the arena.
  • Hits call Feel: hit-stop via Engine.time_scale and shake via Camera3D.h_offset / v_offset, with the numbers in the design section.
  • Waves are exactly (3, 0), (4, 1), (5, 2), (6, 3), spawned from six Marker3D nodes. 2.5 s gap. Clear bonus 200 * wave.
  • HUD shows health, wave, and score. Esc pauses. Death and victory use the same card with Retry and Quit.
  • Groups used at runtime: player, enemies, feel.
  • F5 can be played from the first spawn through a win, and through a death plus Retry, without errors in the Output panel.

│   ├── AnimationPlayer
│   ├── AnimationTree
│   └── Health                          [health.gd]
├── Feel (Node)                         [feel.gd]  group "feel"
└── HUD (CanvasLayer)                   [hud.gd]  process Always
PieceResponsibility
main.gdWaves, spawn offsets, score, register_kill, notify_player_died. Signals: wave_changed, score_changed, game_finished.
player.gdCamera, camera-relative move_and_slide(), jump, attacks, dodge, i-frames, travel() on the state machine.
health.gdclass_name Health. take_damage, heal, signals health_changed and died.
chaser.gd / spitter.gdGroup enemies. Path following. Melee area or projectile. Report score on death.
projectile.gdArea3D plus a ray on layers world and player, because CSG collision does not emit body_entered.
feel.gdGroup feel. hit_stop(scale, real_seconds) and shake(amount). Process Mode Always.
hud.gdBar and labels. Pause, resume, retry, quit. Listens to Health and to Main.

No autoloads. Collision bits, if you set them in code: world 1, player 2, enemies 4, player_attacks 8, enemy_attacks 16. The player body scans world and enemies. The spring arm scans world only and excludes the player's RID.
