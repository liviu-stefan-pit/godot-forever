Training game 5 · 3D · First-person dungeon crawler

Torchlight Dungeon

A one-floor GridMap dungeon, lit by the lamp on your head. Shoot two guards, find a brass key and an iron key, open the matching doors, and step on the green exit. Your best time is kept between runs.

Built on Days 12–13GridMap, RayCast3D, NavigationAgent3D, SubViewport, volumetric fog

Game design

You spawn in a closed stone room with a warm spotlight and a short omni fill. The rest of the level is almost black: distance fog plus a thin volumetric fog so the beam shows in the air. Movement is first person. The body yaws, the head pitches, the mouse is captured until you pause or the run ends. Walk is 4.2 m/s. Sprint is 7.0 m/s. There is no jump.

The pistol is hitscan. Each click sends one ray, 28 m, from the camera, excluding the player body. Damage is 14. The magazine holds 8. Reserve ammo is 24. A shot cannot fire again for 0.32 s. Reload takes 1.15 s and pulls from the reserve until the magazine is full or the reserve is empty. An empty click starts a reload. Shots hit the world layer and the enemies layer, so a closed door stops a bullet.

Two enemies, 40 health each, move at 2.6 m/s on the floor navigation mesh. They chase only while a sight ray of 12 m hits you. A door or wall on the world layer breaks that ray, so they do not path through a locked door even though the baked mesh still connects the cells. Touch damage is 12, then a 0.9 s cooldown. Your health is 100, so standing still on an enemy is death in about eight seconds. Three hits kill a guard (42 damage).

Two keys, ids brass and iron. Walk into the sphere or press E while the 2.2 m interact ray touches it. Each door stores the same id and opens only when your key list contains it. Opening disables the collision shape on a deferred call and hides the mesh. The exit at the south-east corner fires only when you hold both keys. Fewer than two keys shows a prompt and does not end the run.

The clock runs in _process on Main, so pause freezes it. Death and escape pause the tree, release the mouse, and show a panel with Play again. Escape reloads the current scene. A faster escape overwrites user://torchlight_best.json (best_msec, cast back with int() because JSON returns floats). A slower escape leaves the file alone.

Scope: what "done" means

This is a training game. Done means the list below, played in one F5 session from main.tscn. It does not mean menus, localization, or a store build. Those start with the Ship Kit on Day 15.

  • Project C:\GodotProjects\g05-torchlight-dungeon uses the Forward+ renderer.
  • Window size is 1280 by 720. Main scene is res://main.tscn.
  • 3D physics layers are named world, player, enemies, player_attacks, enemy_attacks, pickups, triggers.
  • MeshLibrary exports Floor and Wall from primitive BoxMeshes, materials on the mesh resources.
  • Floor item has a StaticBody3D and a baked NavigationMesh (agent radius 0.4, height 1.6).
  • Wall item has collision and no navigation region.
  • GridMap cell size is 2, 2, 2, Center Y is off, Bake Navigation is on, and the ASCII map above is painted.
  • WorldEnvironment uses a custom background color, low ambient light, distance fog, and volumetric fog.
  • First-person body: yaw on the CharacterBody3D, pitch on Head, mouse captured, sprint at 7.0 m/s, walk at 4.2 m/s.
  • SpotLight3D (range 14, angle 26, shadows on) and OmniLight3D (range 3.2, shadows off) flicker on the head.
  • Footsteps play from a generated click while you move on the floor. Walk step 0.48 s, sprint step 0.32 s.
  • Escape pauses, shows the cursor, and resumes. The run timer does not advance while paused.
  • Hitscan damage is 14, magazine 8, reserve 24, reload 1.15 s, ray length 28 m, mask world plus enemies.
  • Two enemies, 40 health, speed 2.6, aggro 12 m with line of sight, touch damage 12 every 0.9 s.
  • Brass and iron keys can be taken by walking into them or by pressing E on the interact ray.
  • Each door opens only for its key id, then its collision is disabled and the mesh hides.
  • HUD shows health, ammo, keys out of 2, time, best time, a crosshair, and the interact prompt.
  • Minimap is a 256 SubViewport, Own World 3D off, Update Mode Always, orthogonal Camera3D size 18, drawn on a TextureRect.
  • At 0 health the run ends, the cursor returns, and Play again reloads the scene.
  • The exit requires both keys, writes user://torchlight_best.json when the time is better, and shows that best time.

Assets and credits

No art pack and no audio file. Floors, walls, the player blip, enemies, keys, doors, and the exit disc are BoxMesh, CapsuleMesh, SphereMesh, or CylinderMesh with StandardMaterial3D colors set on the mesh resource. Footsteps are an AudioStreamWAV built in code (8-bit, 22050 Hz, a short decaying sine). The only credit line this game needs, if you add a credits label later:

Built with Godot Engine
https://godotengine.org

Godot's own license ships with the editor. You did not download a third-party asset, so there is no Kenney line and no font line.

Stretch goals

  • A third enemy that patrols two Marker3D points until the sight ray hits the player. The Day 13 challenge is this, with damage left at 12.
  • Two OmniLight3D sconces with a phase offset, energy flicker only, so volumetric fog does not trail a moving light. The Day 12 challenge places them.
  • A second floor: another GridMap at y = 2, a ramp cell you author as a third library item, and a second exit that does not count until floor 1 is cleared. Rebake navigation and re-export the library.
  • Replace the generated footstep with a CC0 click you record yourself. Keep the player on the AudioStreamPlayer you already have. Do not add an autoload just for one sound.
  • A gamepad look using the right stick inside _physics_process, added as extra events on the existing actions. Do not rename the actions.
  • Muzzle flash: a 0.05 s energy spike on the SpotLight3D inside _fire. Skip a second shadow-casting light.
