Idle Clicker: Crumb Foundry

A Cookie-Clicker-style foundry in a 720×1280 portrait window. Tap for crumbs, buy generators from resource files, come back to offline pay, and prestige lifetime earnings into sparks. The course title is Idle Clicker; the phone shows Crumb Foundry.

Days 17–18Ship Kit · Resources · SaveManager

Game design

One screen. The player taps a large button, then spends crumbs on generators that produce crumbs per second, then on upgrades that multiply taps, one generator, or everything. Closing the app still pays, at half rate, for at most 8 hours. Prestige wipes the run and pays sparks from lifetime crumbs. There is no game-over. The session ends when the player opens the Ship Kit pause menu or the Menu button.

Controls. Mouse or touch on the tap button and the rows. The pause action opens pause_menu.tscn. No new Input Map actions.

Currencies. Crumbs and lifetime are floats, saved as strings. Owned counts and total_clicks are small ints, cast with int() on load. Sparks are floats too. Nothing in this game is sent through Events.score_changed or Events.game_over, because those signals take an int.

Formulas

Next unit when you already own n: ceilf(base_cost * growth ^ n). Buying k at once: ceilf(base_cost * growth ^ n * (growth ^ k - 1) / (growth - 1)). One sprout from zero is 15; the next is 18; ten from zero cost 305.

Per second: sum(base_rate * owned * generator_mult) * global_mult. A tap adds click_mult * global_mult (base click is 1) and does not change the rate label.

Offline: rate * min(elapsed_seconds, 28800) * 0.5. Gaps under 5 seconds are ignored.

Sparks ever earned: floorf(sqrt(lifetime / 1e6)). This prestige pays that total - sparks_claimed. Each unspent spark is +2% global (1 + 0.02 * unspent). Spent sparks do not keep the 2%; they keep the permanent upgrade instead.

Crumb upgrades: Steel Tap and Double Tap, click ×2, costs 100 and 500 (Double Tap requires Steel Tap). Sprout Food, Grove Song, and Mill Gears double that generator at 250, 1500, and 12000, and require one of that generator. Focus is global ×1.5 at 50000 and does not persist. Spark upgrades that persist: Ledger (1 spark, global ×1.10), Archive (5, global ×1.25), Monument (25, click ×3).

Assets and credits

No image pack is required. The screen is a ColorRect, labels, and buttons using res://ui/theme/game_theme.tres. The click is an AudioStreamWAV built in code (16-bit, 22050 Hz, 0.08 s). Optional later: a UI click from a Kenney audio pack on kenney.nl/assets (CC0; pick by ear, filenames change between pack versions). Credit line if you add one: "UI sounds: Kenney (www.kenney.nl), CC0". Godot's own license files stay in res://licenses/ from the Ship Kit. Do not add an ad network or a billing plugin in the course build.
