Deep Digger

An endless landscape miner. Fly a drill through noise-carved rock, spend fuel, fill a cargo bay, and sell at the surface. Deeper rock pays more and hits harder. The Ship Kit supplies the menu, pause, settings, audio buses, and save autoload. This page is the design contract for Days 27 and 28.

Days 27–28TileMapLayer, FastNoiseLite, CharacterBody2D1280×720 landscape

Game design

You drop a three-cell shaft through a dirt crust and dig until the tank or the hull says stop. Ore is the only thing that occupies cargo. The surface refills fuel at 40 per second and hull at 8 per second. Selling is a button, not a timer. Hull failure is the loss condition for a dive: cargo is wiped, the drill returns to the shaft, credits and upgrades remain.

Controls, all from the Ship Kit input map: move_left, move_right, move_up, move_down (WASD and arrows) fly the drill. Holding a direction into a solid cell drills it. interact (E) opens and closes the shop when the drill's cell Y is 2 or less; underground it only toasts that the shop is on the surface. pause (Escape) is the Ship Kit pause menu. The drill uses MOTION_MODE_FLOATING at 110 pixels per second, or 44 when fuel is empty, and it will not dig at empty.

Window: 1280×720, stretch mode canvas_items, aspect expand, Mobile renderer. Package id com.yourname.deepdigger. Localization prefix DIGGER_. Project folder C:\GodotProjects\deep-digger, copied from the Ship Kit. Autoloads stay Events, Settings, SaveManager, Audio, SceneRouter. Do not redefine them.

Formulas

depth = max(0, cell_y - 2). Surface is any cell with cell_y <= 2.

Fuel burn per second while thrusting or while the dig timer is advancing. Holding still costs nothing. Gas multiplies this by 3.

(1.6 + 0.035 * depth) * (1 - 0.08 * efficiency_level)

On the frame a tile breaks, also subtract hardness * 0.8. The break is refused when fuel is below that cost, when the tile is bedrock, or when the tile is ore and cargo is full.

Dig seconds: hardness / (1 + 0.35 * drill_level). Move speed is 110, times 0.4 when fuel is 0.

Sell payout is the sum, per ore, of floor(units * unit_value * (1 + 0.10 * assay_level)). Ten copper is 80 credits with assay 0, which is the first fuel tank.

Upgrade cost from level L to L + 1 is base * (L + 1) * (L + 1).

Worked tank prices: 80, 320, 720, 1280, 2000. Hull prices stop at 2400 (level 3 to 4). Assay prices: 200, 800, 1800.

Pressure, only when depth > 40: (depth - 40) * 0.15 * (1 - 0.20 * hull_level) hull per second. Magma: 18 hull per second while the drill's cell is magma. At hull 0, cargo is cleared, fuel becomes 20% of max, hull becomes 35% of max, the drill moves to cell (0, 1), Events.game_over emits the credit total, and the slot saves. Darkness alpha is clamp(depth / 120, 0, 0.72).

Caves: cave noise above 0.32 is air. Ore noise places crystal below depth 80 above 0.72, gold below 48 above 0.62, iron below 24 above 0.50, copper below 8 above 0.42. Magma is cave noise below -0.75 past depth 30. Gas is cave noise from 0.20 to 0.32 past depth 18. Bedrock is a thin vein where the absolute cave value is under 0.015 past depth 16. Cave frequency is 0.045, ore frequency 0.09, both TYPE_SIMPLEX_SMOOTH and FRACTAL_FBM.

Scope: what "done" means

Store-ready means the list below, not a content update. Stretch goals are not part of the two days.

  • Ship Kit main menu, settings (volume, fullscreen, locale), and pause menu still work. Autoload scripts were not rewritten.
  • F5 opens the menu. Play uses SceneRouter and lands in res://game/game.tscn.
  • Drill flies, digs, refuels on the surface, and refuses bedrock.
  • Shop sells at the formula and buys the six upgrades at the table. First tank is 80 credits.
  • Magma and pressure can fail a dive without deleting credits or upgrades.
  • Chunks unload past a radius of 2. Dug cells stay empty when the chunk loads again.
  • SaveManager.save_slot(&"run", data) restores seed, cargo, upgrades, tutorial step, and holes. Profile keys best_depth and best_credits use set_value. Counters are cast with int() on load.
  • Save also runs about every 8 seconds and on focus-out, app pause, and window close.
  • DIGGER_ strings exist for en, es, and de. The other Ship Kit locale columns are filled, even where they still match English.
  • Tutorial lines advance from first dig, to first ore, to a return from depth past 8, to opening the shop.
  • Credits scene and licenses/THIRD_PARTY.txt name the generated atlas. Godot's license files are still present.
  • Windows release exe at C:\GodotProjects\deep-digger\build\DeepDigger.exe keeps a dug cell across a full quit.
  • Android debug build uses package com.yourname.deepdigger, landscape, and reaches the mine. Version 1.0.0.

├── HUD (CanvasLayer, layer 1)     [hud.gd, class_name DiggerHud]
│   ├── Darkness (ColorRect)       unique name
│   ├── MarginContainer
│   │   └── VBoxContainer
│   │       ├── FuelLabel, FuelBar
│   │       ├── HullLabel, HullBar
│   │       ├── InfoLabel, DigLabel
│   │       └── TutorialLabel
│   └── Shop (Panel)               [shop.gd, class_name DiggerShop]
│       └── buttons: Sell, Tank, Drill, Cargo, Hull, Efficiency, Assay, Close
└── PauseMenu                      instance of res://ui/pause_menu.tscn, layer 20

DiggerGame owns the numbers and the slot. DiggerTerrain owns noise, set_cell, erase_cell, and the dug dictionary. DiggerDrill owns movement and the dig timer. The HUD and the shop only read and call the game. Group digger_game is added in code. Group player is how the terrain finds who to stream around. Chunks are 16×16. Source id is 0 unless the TileSet panel shows another atlas id. Physics layer: tiles on world, drill on player masked to world. Columns 6 and 7 have no collision polygon.

Slot &"run" stores seed, credits, fuel, hull, cargo, upgrades, tutorial step, best depth, dug list as [[x, y], …], and player position as [x, y]. Events.game_started, score_changed, game_over, toast_requested, and achievement_unlocked with id digger_depth_50 are emitted. They are not redeclared.

Assets and credits

The mine is a generated atlas: nine 16×16 rectangles drawn by make_tiles.gd into res://assets/art/tiles.png. No download is required. The drill is a Polygon2D triangle. Optional hit audio is an exported AudioStream on the drill, played with Audio.play_sfx_varied. Leave it empty to ship silent.

External resource: Kenney

If you replace the squares, use kenney.nl/assets (CC0). Search for a top-down or abstract tilesheet and pick the file by looking at it. Pack versions rename files, so this course does not name one. The Godot tileset tutorial's example pack is Kenney's Abstract Platformer. Rebuild collision if the tile size is not 16. Verify the license on the pack page before you ship.
